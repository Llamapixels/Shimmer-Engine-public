Player and actor hits are now separate, with independent collision, interaction and damage controls.

- Actor and prefab scripts have On Player Hit and On Hit 1–4 tabs, with enable switches and legacy On Hit compatibility.
- Set Actor Hit Response enables or disables contact and projectile-group responses during play.
- Actors can be non-solid while remaining grounded, interactable and hittable.
- Safe Pickup actors collect once through On Collect, either on touch or with Collect Actor from an interaction/script.
- Prefabs support drag-and-drop onto scene/world maps, visible placement controls and a shortcut for adding Spawn Prefab to scene start.
- Named animation states resolve on the actor's current sprite sheet, including alternate player sprites and custom-script actor inputs.

Existing projects: move player-contact damage from actor On Hit to the appropriate On Player Hit handler. In platformer scenes, Auto solidity now follows the Platform checkbox; explicitly select Solid for actors that must block on all sides. Rebuild ROMs after updating.

Validation: editor type checks and production build, native collision/state/pickup regressions, custom-script compiler tests, prefab placement/store tests and two GBA ROM builds passed locally. Interactive emulator/device playtesting remains necessary.

Installers: Windows x64, Linux x64 (AppImage and deb), and macOS Apple Silicon.
